The hub has one new action, progress. It refuses to record anything until the round has been acknowledged, so a card cannot appear for feedback nobody has read.

After that check, the action has two forms. steps replaces the whole list with one to twelve titles, each unique and at most eighty characters, because the card is a list the reader scans, not a log. done names one declared title and ticks it. An unknown title is an error, not a no-op.
