The hub adds its own first and last steps around the agent's, so the card reads as one sequence instead of a list that starts in the middle. The reader sees the round from the moment the feedback is read to the moment the revision lands.

sequenceDiagram participant A as agent participant H as hub participant P as page A->>H: ack the round A->>H: progress --steps H-->>P: reading the feedback H-->>P: the three steps A->>H: progress --done H-->>P: first step ticked A->>H: publish H-->>P: revision lands

The page polls the same status endpoint it always did. The card is one more field in the response, and the frame updates it in place, one segment and one row per step, so a tick does not redraw the card.
