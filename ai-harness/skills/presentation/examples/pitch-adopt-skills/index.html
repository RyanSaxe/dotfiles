Adopt agent skills Pitch 4 / 10
Definition

A "skill" is a packaged workflow.

Property 01

One folder. Versioned in git.

Lives next to your code. Reviewed like your code. Changes show up in PRs.

Property 02

Triggered by a name or phrase.

Anyone can invoke /migration-review. No tribal-knowledge tax.

Property 03

States its inputs and outputs.

What you give it, what it produces, how to verify. A first-class artifact.

Not a chat snippet. Not a prompt library. A first-class artifact in our repos.

Adopt agent skills Pitch 6 / 10
Proposal

Three phases. One quarter.

  1. Phase 1 — Seed

    Port 5 high-leverage workflows already in someone's head into shared skills. Land them in the platform repo with reviews. 2 weeks.

  2. Phase 2 — Adopt

    Roll out to one team. Measure invocations, time saved, and what breaks. Iterate weekly with the team. 4 weeks.

  3. Phase 3 — Scale

    Open contribution. Anyone can author a skill. Review process matches code review. Public skill catalog. 6 weeks.
